# OpenEvidence

Founded 2022 by Daniel Nadler (previously Kensho Technologies, sold to S&P Global
for $700M) and Zachary Ziegler (CTO). Based in Miami, FL. An AI-powered clinical
decision support platform -- a medical search engine that takes a clinician's
question or patient case and returns answers grounded in peer-reviewed literature,
with citations. HIPAA-compliant. Free for verified US healthcare professionals.
As of January 2026, valued at $12B -- the most highly valued healthcare AI company
in the world.
