# EU Medical Body Partnerships

OpenEvidence built its content moat through official AI content partnerships with US
medical societies ([[Medical Society Network]]). No equivalent partnerships with
European medical bodies -- EMA, national associations, European specialist colleges --
were ever established. This absence is both a cause and a consequence of the
[[EU-UK Withdrawal]]: without European content credentialing, the [[Medical Literature
Corpus]] had no European institutional endorsement; without European institutional
relationships, there was no stakeholder pressure to remain through regulatory
compliance. The two absences reinforce each other.

Required-by any credible European re-entry · never-built by [[OpenEvidence]] ·
research question: which European medical bodies would be the right first-mover
partnership targets if OpenEvidence chose to re-enter?

- Why this matters: this is the structural gap that any European competitor or
  re-entry strategy must address first.
