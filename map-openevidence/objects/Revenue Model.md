# Revenue Model

The [[Free Clinician Access Model]] is explicit. The monetization path is not.
Two disclosed routes: enterprise contracts with [[Enterprise Health Systems]] (no
pricing disclosed) and the [[Open Vista Suite]] pharma product co-developed with
[[Veeva Systems]] (not yet generating reported revenue as of April 2026).
OpenEvidence raised $700M in 12 months on a free-to-clinician model at a $12B
valuation -- the gap between capital raised and revenue disclosed is wide.
The ghost is not that revenue does not exist; it is that the mechanism is not
publicly confirmed. This matters structurally: if Open Vista does not launch or
underperforms, the monetization logic of the entire [[VC Investor Coalition]]
bet becomes exposed.

research question: what are the actual enterprise contract economics, and has
