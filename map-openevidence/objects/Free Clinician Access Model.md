# Free Clinician Access Model

The foundational go-to-market decision: individual access is free for any verified
US healthcare professional (NPI verification required). No institutional purchase,
no subscription fee. This drove the 40%+ US physician adoption rate -- growth that
would not have been possible behind a paywall. The trade-off is that it defers
monetization to the enterprise ([[Enterprise Health Systems]] contracts) and the
pharma layer ([[Open Vista Suite]]), creating the [[Revenue Model]] ghost for the
core product. The model also made the [[EU-UK Withdrawal]] lower-cost: no paying
individual European subscribers were lost.

Made-by [[OpenEvidence]] · enables scale of [[US Clinician Base]] ·
defers monetization to [[Open Vista Suite]] · creates [[Revenue Model]] ghost

- Hits: physician adoption speed, network effects, clinical trust.
- Does not hit: short-term revenue; the model requires the [[Open Vista Suite]]
  or enterprise contracts to close the monetization loop.
