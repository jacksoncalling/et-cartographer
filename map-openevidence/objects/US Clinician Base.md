# US Clinician Base

The primary user population: over 40% of all US physicians use OpenEvidence daily
as of 2026, across more than 10,000 hospitals. 65,000 new clinician registrations
per month. User types include attending physicians (primary care, internal medicine,
family medicine, psychiatry most cited), residents, and registered nurses with NPIs.
The platform is free and requires NPI verification -- no institutional purchase
required for individual access. In a 2026 hospitalist survey at a large urban
academic center, OpenEvidence was used by 51.9% of respondents, more than any other
AI tool.

Served-by [[OpenEvidence]] · accesses [[Clinical Search Engine]] ·
benefits-from [[Medical Society Network]] content ·
embedded-via [[EHR Integration Layer]] in enterprise settings ·
governed-by [[HIPAA]]

- Hits: clinical decision quality, drug selection, rare disease lookup,
  point-of-care protocol checking.
- Does not hit: patients directly; the tool answers the clinician, not the patient.
