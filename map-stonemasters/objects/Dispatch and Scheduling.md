# Dispatch and Scheduling

The capability of assigning crews to jobs, handling sick-cover shuffles, and
deciding which job to stop or change when circumstances shift. Held jointly by
[[Bram]] and [[Niels]]; no one else can exercise it. This is the constraint:
4-5 teams running in parallel, all dispatch decisions funneling through two
people. PlanCraft handles the scheduling mechanics (Termine, Zeiterfassung),
but the judgment of WHO goes WHERE lives in two heads.
