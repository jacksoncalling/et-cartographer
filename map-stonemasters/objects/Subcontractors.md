# Subcontractors

Brought in when too many people are sick or capacity is exceeded. One of the
judgment calls only [[Bram]] and [[Niels]] can make: "When do we get
subcontractors in?" No names, no firms, no frequency cited. They exist as a
live option in the decision tree, not as a named relationship.

engaged-by [[Bram]] or [[Niels]] . supplements [[The Crew]]

- **Hits:** [[On-Site Execution]] (they do the work when internal capacity
  is short), [[Sick Cover Dispatch]] (they are one of the options).
- **Does not hit:** [[Crew Training and Upskilling]]. Subs are temporary
  capacity, not part of the retention/development effort.
