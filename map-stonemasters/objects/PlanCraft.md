# PlanCraft

The firm's central software tool. Handles Termine (scheduling), Zeiterfassung
(time tracking), and Doku (documentation). Described as the operational backbone.
Niels's role is partly tied to it ("Operations, PlanCraft" in Josh's contact
notes). It is infrastructure; the judgment that drives dispatch does not live
in it. PlanCraft holds the mechanics, not the decisions.

used-by [[Bram]], [[Niels]], [[The Crew]] .
supports [[Dispatch and Scheduling]]

- **Hits:** [[Dispatch and Scheduling]] (if PlanCraft broke, scheduling mechanics
  would need a replacement). The tool is necessary but not sufficient.
- **Does not hit:** [[Judgment Stock]], [[Job Quality Visibility]]. PlanCraft
  records what happened (time, schedule) but not why a job went well or badly.
  It is a clock, not a mirror.
