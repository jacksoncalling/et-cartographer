# Refuse Small Jobs

A governance choice made this year (2026): stop taking on every single job,
especially small ones where prep and cleanup time (4 hours) exceeds or matches
on-site time (4 hours). Previously they "nearly took on every single job."
The decision is live and being exercised. It represents a shift from
volume-maximizing to margin-protecting behavior.

decided-by [[Bram]] . depends-on [[Quoting and Intake]] (the filter
applies at intake) . contested-by [[Helping vs Margin]]

- **Hits:** [[Equipment and Kit]] (less prep/cleanup churn), [[The Crew]]
  (fewer context switches), revenue mix (fewer small, more concentrated).
- **Does not hit:** [[Big Clients]]. The decision affects the tail of the
  job portfolio, not the key relationships.

Source: voice note 2026-08-03 ("this year we've decided not to take on every
single job, especially the littler jobs ... two hours to prepare, two hours to
clean, there for four hours, and then there are issues").
