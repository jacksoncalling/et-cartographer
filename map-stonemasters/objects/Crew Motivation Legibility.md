# Crew Motivation Legibility

What motivates the team? What could we do to motivate them more? Why does
[[Kai]] always come late? Bram says "I know a fair bit about it, but
some of them I don't." This is a named desire for legibility into crew behavior
and motivation, with no wiring: no instrument, no ritual, no feedback loop
captures it.

Note from the interview guide: the richest data for this may sit with a team
lead or with Niels, not with Bram. The blind side is not just unseen; it may
be unsee-able from the owner's vantage point alone.

**Ghost because:** named explicitly, referenced as opportunity #2 in the
outcome-note, but no source shows a tool, a conversation structure, a ritual,
or any mechanism that makes crew motivation visible.
