# Judgment Stock

The accumulated operational judgment that lives in [[Bram]] and [[Niels]]'s
heads: which crew to put where, which job to drop, when quality will be an issue,
what motivates the team, which clients to cultivate. Everyone references it (it
is how dispatch works, how jobs get accepted, how sick cover happens), but it has
no external representation: no document, no protocol, no rubric, no system makes
it legible to anyone else. It is the most-connected ghost in this map.

**Ghost because:** named implicitly in every operational decision, but no source
gives it a holder other than two people's memories, no instrument captures it,
and no tool (including [[PlanCraft]]) represents it. It is the "blind side"
Bram named.

UNVERIFIED - research: How do comparable trade firms (rope access, scaffolding,
specialized industrial services) externalize operational judgment when scaling
past the owner-operator stage? What form does it take: checklists, dispatch
rubrics, post-job reviews, crew debriefs, something else?

held-by nobody (ghost)

- **Hits:** everything operational. This is the connective tissue between the
  governance cluster and the operations cluster. If it disappeared (owner
  incapacitated, partner leaves), the bridge collapses.
- **Does not hit:** [[PlanCraft]] (the tool is a clock; this ghost is the
  judgment the clock cannot record).
