# Sick Cover Dispatch

The recurring decision: when someone is sick, who do we put where? What job do
we stop, change, or staff with [[Subcontractors]]? Made by [[Bram]] and
[[Niels]] exclusively. This is not a one-time choice but a repeating judgment
call that fires every time capacity drops unexpectedly.

decided-by [[Bram]], [[Niels]] . depends-on [[Judgment Stock]] .
affects [[The Crew]], [[Subcontractors]], [[On-Site Execution]]

- **Hits:** [[The Crew]] (who works where changes), [[Subcontractors]]
  (they get called in), [[On-Site Execution]] (a job may stop or degrade).
- **Does not hit:** [[Big Clients]] directly. The client sees the output
  (job done or delayed), not the internal shuffle.

Source: voice note 2026-08-03 ("if someone's sick, how do we adapt? ... who do
we put where? What job site would we choose not to do? If too many people are
sick, which job do we stop? Which job do we change? When do we get
subcontractors in?").
