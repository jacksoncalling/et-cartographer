# Kai

Named crew member who "always comes late." Bram uses him as the concrete
instance of the [[Crew Motivation Legibility]] ghost: "Why does he always come
late? Why do we always need to give him a kick up the arse?" Kai is not a
problem to solve; he is the one named data point where the opacity of crew
behavior becomes visible to Bram.
