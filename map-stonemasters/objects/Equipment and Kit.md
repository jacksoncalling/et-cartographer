# Equipment and Kit

The rope-access equipment shared across teams. Mentioned specifically in the
context of small-job economics: "two hours to prepare for the job, then maybe
two hours to clean our equipment after the job." The prep/cleanup overhead is
what makes small jobs unprofitable relative to their on-site time. This is
physical infrastructure, not decision-making.
