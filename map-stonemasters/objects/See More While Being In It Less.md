# See More While Being In It Less

A structural conflict at the heart of Bram's stated position: he wants to
**see more** of what is really going on (why jobs run smooth vs. messy, what
motivates the crew) AND simultaneously wants to **be in it less** (step out of
daily operations, grow modestly, not be the single point of failure).

These two usually pull against each other. The one thing that bridges them is
making the business legible enough that it does not all have to run through his
and Niels's heads. He sees more precisely BECAUSE he is standing back, not in
spite of it. But that legibility does not exist yet (see [[Judgment Stock]],
[[Job Quality Visibility]], [[Crew Motivation Legibility]]).

Both sides are live: the desire to see is expressed in his own words; the
desire to step back is expressed in his own words. Neither can be fully
satisfied by the other disappearing.
