# Helping vs Margin

A structural conflict in job-acceptance decisions: one decision axis is "does it
help the person? are we helping someone? because that's a big thing, we jump in
a lot to help out." The other axis is effort-vs-reward: "how much energy we put
into the job, how much do we get out." These pull against each other. Helping
means taking jobs that do not pay their prep/cleanup cost. Protecting margin
means refusing them (see [[Refuse Small Jobs]]).

Both sides are live and named by Bram in the same breath. The decision to
refuse small jobs this year is a deliberate tilt toward margin after years of
tilting toward helping. Neither side has won; both still pull.
