# Quoting and Intake

The capability of receiving a request and turning it into a quote with an
effort/reward judgment. Involves deciding how much energy the job will take
vs. how much comes back. Bram describes this as "a feeling I've developed
over the years." A second decision axis: "Does it help the person? Are we
helping someone? Because that's a big thing." This year, the decision filter
tightened (see [[Refuse Small Jobs]]).

- **Hits:** [[Dispatch and Scheduling]] (what gets quoted gets scheduled),
  [[Refuse Small Jobs]] (the filter lives here), [[Big Clients]].
- **Does not hit:** [[Crew Training and Upskilling]]. Quote decisions do not
  touch the development layer.
