# The Crew

~16 employees (8 full-time, 8 part-time), organised into 4-5 rope-access teams.
Includes team leads (unnamed in sources except [[Kai]]), students (seasonal),
and trained workers. The firm is investing in upskilling ("higher skilled workers
now") and sees training as a retention tool. Industry has student/seasonal churn.

Holds [[On-Site Execution]] . depends-on [[Equipment and Kit]] .

- **Hits:** [[On-Site Execution]], [[Equipment and Kit]], [[Crew Motivation Legibility]].

- **Does not hit:** [[Winning Work]]. Crew does not touch client acquisition;
  that is Bram's relationship network.
