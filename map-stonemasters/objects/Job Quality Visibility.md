# Job Quality Visibility

Bram's stated "blind side": why do some jobs run smoothly and others have
issues? This is named, desired, and has no wiring. No instrument, no record, no
process, no tool captures what made a job go well or badly after the fact.
PlanCraft records scheduling and time, not quality or cause. The information
presumably exists in the crew's experience of each job, but nothing makes it
legible to Bram or anyone else.

**Ghost because:** named explicitly by the owner, referenced in the outcome-note
as opportunity #1 (the bullseye), but no source shows a holder, a tool, a ritual,
or a record that actually captures it.
